
===================
The Bobcat-sdk Book
===================

.. image:: ../logo.svg
   :width: 100px

The Bobcat-sdk Book is a guide on developing with `bobcat-sdk`, a Rust SDK for building
Arbitrum-based Web3 dApps. It assumes familiarity with Rust and Solidity.
